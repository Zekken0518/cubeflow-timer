Install on iPhone / iOS

CubeFlow is built as an offline-capable iPhone Progressive Web App:

1 Open this app in Safari on your iPhone.
2 Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
3 Scroll down and select Add to Home Screen.

Once added, it launches full-screen without Safari address bars and stores all your solves locally on your iPhone.
